# AirCare – Mental Health App (UI/UX Case Study)

A mental health app that offers private, flexible access to support for
people with demanding lifestyles, with personalized therapist matching,
progress tracking, and mood-based exercise recommendations.

**Final project** · Nancy Saad · Assiut University

## The Problem

Many people, like Sarah Ahmed, a nurse working in a high-stress
environment, struggle to access consistent mental health support. Busy
schedules and societal stigma make traditional therapy hard to fit into
their lives.

## The Solution

A private, flexible, and accessible platform that fits into users' lives,
so they can take control of their mental well-being without sacrificing
time, privacy, or convenience.

## Key Features

**Current**
- User authentication (sign up, login, password recovery)
- Flexible therapy sessions (book, reschedule, cancel, last-minute options)
- Therapist matching algorithm
- Progress tracking (mood tracker, goal setting, progress reports)
- Resources (articles, guides, meditation and relaxation exercises)
- Profile management

**Future**
- Live chat support
- Community support groups
- AI-powered chatbot
- Gamification elements
- Customizable therapy plans
